A note from me

I've always been the person who wants to know your story.

That curiosity is what pulled me into Human and Organizational Development. I'm fascinated by how people shape the places they work, and how those places shape them back. It's also why I started Empowering Immigrants in high school, and why my honors thesis asks how college experiences shape the careers that follow.

Executive search brings all of it together. I get to sit with a company and understand what it really needs, sit with a candidate and understand what they really want, and then find the place where both stories fit.

Thank you for stopping by. I'd love to hear yours.
